# Soil type findings

- Soil classes in this grid: sand 5 cells, loamy sand 90 cells, sandy loam 55 cells, silt loam 99 cells; 12 cells are reserved (mostly coastal).
- 17 of 33 districts are dominated by silt loam, including the wettest ones: Valsad, The Dangs, Chhota Udaipur, Tapi, Surat.
- Kachchh has about 74% sandy loam and 3% sand by grid cell, the most sandy district in the state.
- Mean total rain per grid cell by soil class: Loamy sand 525 mm; Sand 358 mm; Sandy loam 431 mm; Silt loam 726 mm. Soil and location are mixed together here, so these differences do not show that soil causes the rain pattern.
- Correlation between district runoff susceptibility and flood-risk index: 0.23 (assumption-based susceptibility).
